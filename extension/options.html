Flip-ly Free Finds — Settings

Capture runs in your own browser session and is triggered by you. These settings control which groups count and what gets forwarded.

Connection

Where captured posts are sent. The token must match the server's FB_INGEST_TOKEN.

Groups

Groups are added automatically the first time you open them with the extension active. Turn a group off to stop it forwarding even if you click the button.

    Location

    Distance is a soft filter: a post is only dropped when it contains a real ZIP that resolves beyond your radius. Posts with no ZIP always pass — the group is the main geographic scope.

    Filters

    Applied in your browser before anything is sent — cuts noise and AI cost. One keyword per line or comma-separated.

    Only capture posts that look like giveaways (contain "free", "curb alert", "giving away")
